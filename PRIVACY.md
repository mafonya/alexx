# Snippy Privacy Policy

Snippy stores the snippets and categories you create on your device. The app and keyboard share them through an Apple App Group container.

Snippy does not create accounts, use analytics or advertising, send snippets or typing activity to a server, or share your data with third parties.

Full Access lets the keyboard read your personal snippets from the shared container. Without it, the keyboard offers built-in examples. The keyboard does not transmit what you type.

You can delete snippets and categories in the app. Removing the app removes its locally stored data, subject to any device backups you control.

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/mafonya/snippy/issues.
